\section{The Architectural Duality: DWT and DMT}
Deterministic closed-loop control over a 100-mile turbulent grid is achieved by separating scale-invariant trends from localized geometric bottlenecks, eliminating all statistical computing.

\subsection{The Wavelet Scaling Invariant}
The weather advection field is governed by non-linear Navier-Stokes equations, presenting a multi-scale disturbance space $U$. We apply a Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) as a deterministic algebraic filter. The DWT decomposes the field into scale approximations:
\begin{equation}
\psi_{j,k}(x,y) = 2^{-j/2} \psi(2^{-j}x - k_x, 2^{-j}y - k_y)
\end{equation}
The wavelet transform extracts the macro-system scale trends (low-frequency components) needed to anticipate global battery decay profiles. However, it does not locate the exact coordinates of micro-turbulence cells due to temporal-spatial frequency localization bounds.

\subsection{Discrete Morse Coordinate Pinpointing}
To map the exact spatial positions of barriers and line-of-sight signal occlusions, a non-degenerate Discrete Morse Function $f: K \to \mathbb{R}$ is evaluated over the communication complex $\mathcal{G}_C$. While the wavelets provide the scaling envelope, the Morse function uncovers the precise coordinate locations of the critical simplices:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Critical Minima Vertices ($c_0$):} Coordinate cells tracking total network isolation traps.
    \item \textbf{Critical Saddle Edges ($c_1$):} The exact spatial windows—such as the verified tunnel $(0,2)$—where multi-hop communication lines bypass non-convex obstacles.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Delay-Tolerant Store-and-Forward Buffering}
When an asset enters a line-of-sight occlusion zone mapped by the Morse function, a store-and-forward capability caches visual sensor data locally within a computational queue state $Q_{\text{local}}$. The Self-Similar Permutation Group pauses the active routing word transaction until the drone's trajectory slides along the V-path gradient streamlines and re-enters the coordinates of a valid critical saddle tunnel, flushing the cached data packet with zero loss.
