\subsection{The Continuous Route to Chaos Swing Matrix}

\begin{equation}

\end{equation}
where $m_i$ represents the rotational turbine inertia, $d_i$ represents the natural system damping, and $K_{ij}$ defines the admittance coupling strength of the physical transmission lines. \begin{equation}
