A routing algorithm maps out a grid of connected graph nodes. 
The system initializes a queue to manage the sequence of paths to explore. 
To ensure safety and prevent infinite loops, it tracks explored coordinates in a visited set. 
The pathfinding executor pops the first item from the queue, checks its neighbors, and appends new targets to the back of the line. 
When the queue size drops to zero, the search terminates with a successful shortest path.
