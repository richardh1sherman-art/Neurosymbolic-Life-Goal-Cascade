Time first household child [less than 13 years old] woke up (Note: A value of -3 indicates 'Refused' and a value of -2 indicates 'Don't know.' Values of this variable were converted by the ARDA to decimal form based on the 24-hour clock. For example, a value of 0 is equivalent to 12 a.m., a value of 7 is equivalent to 7 a.m., and a value of 15.50 is equivalent to 3:30 p.m. To calculate minutes on the hour, multiply decimal values by 60 [e.g., 0.50 x 60 = 30 minutes].)
