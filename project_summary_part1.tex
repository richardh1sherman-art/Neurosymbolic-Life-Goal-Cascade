\section{Introduction and Architectural Core}
Neuro-Symbolic AI (NeSy) is defined as any system that combines the sub-symbolic pattern-recognition capabilities of deep learning (neural networks) with the logical, rule-based reasoning of traditional computer science (symbolic AI). Our research implements a multi-tier pipeline explicitly configured to satisfy five core desiderata: deep qualitative reasoning, robust out-of-distribution generalization, complete semantic interpretability, efficient sample learning from sparse data exemplars, and transactional domain transferability.

The unified system architecture is organized across four distinct operational layers:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Intent:} The teleological definition of narrative, spiritual, and multi-agent social structures.
    \item \textbf{Specifications:} The input-output interface mappings and vocabulary constraints bounding the environment.
    \item \textbf{Context:} The dynamic extraction of situational semantics via interlocking decision forest memories.
    \item \textbf{Training and Inferencing:} The optimization and execution plane where agents plan, reason, and learn under uncertainty.
\end{itemize}
